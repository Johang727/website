Johang the Flareon

Many people's fursonas have different names, but the reason mine is the same.. is because I lowkey wasn't creative enough to think of a different name to go by online. So, guess my legal first name shall be it, although, friends refer to me as just "Flareon".

This will likely be the only unfinished artwork on here and the one with the most yap.

If it seems simple and semi-uncreative, I suppose I could say that's the point. I didn't wanna go with something overly flashy or different, so I decided to go with my favorite Pokémon.

The story behind the fur color is essentially: the flareon on my chatbox had a really light fur color and a streamer told me I should go for a ligher color for a more unique color palate. So I decided to do something in the middle. The mane and tail aren't completely yellow, opting for more of a cream color. The rest of the fur is a bit lighter, but still quite orange.. since I like the color orange :3

He is fluffy btw, it's just a flatcolor reference.

Anyways, once I draw more, there will be more images here.
